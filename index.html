Shirt and a meal

What to wear

Saturday workers get an event T-shirt and a meal.

Wear

  • The event T-shirt, as given (Saturday)
  • Clean shorts, long pants, or jeans
  • Comfortable closed-toe shoes. Sneakers or work boots are best

Don't wear

  • Cutoffs
  • Flip-flops or sandals
  • A cut, ripped, or frayed event shirt

Good to bring: a water bottle, sunscreen, and a hat.

You're representing the team

  • Be courteous and respectful to CRE staff, Vantive guests, and each other.
  • No tobacco or vaping, drugs, alcohol, or foul language anywhere on site.
  • Only crew members on site. No friends, siblings, or rides hanging around.
  • Rain or shine. The event won't be postponed for weather unless CRE tells us.

Break the dress code or conduct rules and CRE will remove you from the site.
